Proposed approach

A disciplined first 90 days

Establish a baseline, test the work with its owners, and give the VP evidence for the next decision.

Days 01–30

Establish the baseline

Confirm the close calendar, exception backlog, system access and donor-report commitments with Finance, the DBA and Stewardship.

Evidence: agreed definitions, owners and baseline.

Days 31–60

Test one complete cycle

Run a parallel close and one scholarship report. Rehearse backup coverage and coordinate list cut-offs with school partners.

Evidence: signed tie-out and reviewed donor report.

Days 61–90

Decide what to scale

Review results with the VP. Adopt, revise or extend the pilot based on verified claims, working controls and available capacity.

Evidence: a documented decision and next steps.

Model contract

Every report states the question, method, inputs, output with uncertainty, validation, the decision it enables and a guardrail. No model rates a person. The operating model follows the four pillars in this proposal — gift processing and fund management, database and systems, stewardship and donor relations, and prospect development and analytics — plus the two enablers, staff management and school-wide partnership.

What is synthetic

All operating figures, trends, forecasts, before/after values, workflow volumes and staffing assumptions are illustrative. Published facts are confined to the anchors and source list. The model guards state the limits explicitly, including the need for a real audit, system extract, holdout test or time-log baseline before any claim is used.

How to read a report

Start with the delivery plan, then the expected outcome, then the limitation. The plan is what I would do; the outcome is what I would measure; the limitation is what this evidence does not prove.
