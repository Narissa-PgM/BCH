Proposed approach

A disciplined first 90 days

Establish a baseline, test the work with its owners, and give the VP evidence for the next decision.

Days 01–30

Establish the baseline

Confirm the close calendar, exception backlog, system access and donor-report commitments with Finance, the DBA and Stewardship.

Evidence: agreed definitions, owners and baseline.

Days 31–60

Test one complete cycle

Run a parallel close and one scholarship report. Rehearse backup coverage and coordinate list cut-offs with school partners.

Evidence: signed tie-out and reviewed donor report.

Days 61–90

Decide what to scale

Review results with the VP. Adopt, revise or extend the pilot based on verified claims, working controls and available capacity.

Evidence: a documented decision and next steps.
