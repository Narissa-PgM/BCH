Three gates, one clear decision

Each gate has an owner and evidence. Day 90 ends with a documented decision, not a status update.

At day 90, the VP chooses one of four outcomes

AdoptTargets met, controls working, capacity available.
ReviseControls work, but a target or workload needs adjusting.
DeferCore service would suffer. Schedule it later.
StopNo measurable benefit, or the risk is too high.
